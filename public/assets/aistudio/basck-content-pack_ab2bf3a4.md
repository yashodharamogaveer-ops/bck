## 2. Founders and history

- **Dr. T. M. A. Pai**: founder of Syndicate Bank, medical and engineering colleges, the Academy of General Education, Manipal and other institutions. He agreed to sponsor a First Grade College at Kundapura under AGE Manipal when Dr. A. S. Bhandarkar made his offer.
- **Dr. A. S. Bhandarkar**: native of Kundapura, practising medicine in Bahrain. He donated Rs. 2,00,000 (his savings). The college carries his family name at the insistence of the citizens of Kundapura and AGE Manipal, as he did not want a name for himself.
- Rotary Club of Kundapura and leading citizens worked for the college's opening, a long-felt need of the taluk.
- Photos: /images/management/tmapai.jpg, /images/management/DrASBhandarkar.jpg
